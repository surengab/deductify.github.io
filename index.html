How We Build

Three rules we don't break.

Your data is yours

Productify stores every habit, goal, and mood entry locally on your iPhone — no accounts, no servers, no sale of your behaviour to anyone. Privacy isn't a settings toggle; it's the default.

Useful in ten seconds

Open, do the one thing you came for, close. No onboarding mazes, no feeds to scroll, no notifications engineered to pull you back in when you don't need us.

Native, not wrapped

Written for iOS with the platform's own tools, so you get Apple Watch, widgets, Live Activities, and the responsiveness you expect from an iPhone app.

The Studio

Small studio, long horizon.

Deductify is an independent iOS studio. We ship few apps and keep them going for years — Productify has been on the App Store since 2018 and is still updated. Nothing we build carries ads, dark patterns, or engagement traps, because the only thing we want from you is that the app is worth keeping on your home screen.
