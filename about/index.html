The Studio

About Deductify

An independent iOS studio building calm, focused apps for everyday life.

What we make

Deductify makes iOS apps for people who want software that works quietly in the background of their day. Each app does one thing well and gets out of your way. We don't design for engagement metrics — we design for actual usefulness, then we ship and listen.

Our two current apps are Productify, a habit and goal tracker that's been on the App Store since 2018 and is still updated, and Plantive, a plant identifier and care companion released in 2026. Both are free, both are built natively for iOS, and both share the same philosophy: clear purpose, no engagement traps, no ads, no data harvesting.

We're a small team, which is a constraint we've learned to treat as an advantage. It means we ship fewer things, we keep them running for years rather than abandoning them, and every message sent to contact@deductify.org reaches someone who actually works on the app.

What we believe

  • Privacy by default. Your data stays on your device unless you explicitly choose otherwise. We don't sell, share, or mine user data.
  • One thing well. We resist feature bloat. Every addition has to pass a simple test: does this actually help someone use the app, or does it just give us another thing to market?
  • No dark patterns. No fake countdowns, no manufactured urgency, no friction-as-marketing. If we want you to upgrade, we tell you directly.
  • Calm by design. Software should respect attention. Our apps don't compete with everything else on your phone — they show up when you need them and disappear when you don't.
  • Built to last. We support apps long-term. The version of Productify you download today will keep working for years, with regular updates, on whatever iOS Apple ships next.
