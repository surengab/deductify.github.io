Plantive

Identify any plant and keep it thriving.

Download on the App Store

Plantive is a plant identification and care app for iPhone. Point your camera at any plant and Plantive identifies it in seconds — then provides care guidance tailored to that specific species: watering schedules, light requirements, humidity preferences, and seasonal tips.

It's built for plant owners who want confident answers when something looks wrong, beginners building a collection without horticultural expertise, and enthusiasts who want organised care records for every plant they own. The goal is to remove guesswork from plant care, not to replace the curiosity that comes with it.

Where Plantive differs from generic plant apps is the combination of fast, accurate identification with actionable, per-species care guidance. You don't just learn what your plant is — you get a personalised schedule to keep it healthy, with reminders built in so nothing slips through the cracks.
