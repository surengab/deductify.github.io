Calory!

A clearer picture of what you eat, one meal at a time.

On the App Store Free iPhone & iPad
Download on the App Store Support

Calory! makes daily food tracking easier. Take a photo or describe a meal to get an AI estimate of its calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. Review and adjust the result before adding it to your diary, or enter food manually whenever that is faster.

Your dashboard keeps calories, macros, water, and goals in one place. Look back through previous days, follow weight trends, and see how consistently you have logged without turning every meal into admin.

What's in the app

AI meal logging

Photograph a meal or describe it in your own words, then review and edit the estimated nutrition before saving.

Calories and macros

Follow calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat against personal daily targets with clear progress rings.

A flexible diary

Search everyday foods, adjust portions, attach photos, and edit or remove entries whenever you need to.

Water and progress

Track hydration, weight trends, diary history, and logging streaks alongside the meals that shaped them.

Private by default

Your food diary, attached photos, weight history, water entries, and personal targets are stored on your device. When you choose AI analysis, only the selected meal photo and description are sent for that estimate; your full diary is not included.
