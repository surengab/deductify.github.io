Productify

A daily habit tracker that stays out of your way.

Download on the App Store

Productify is a daily habit tracker for iPhone built around a simple idea: consistency, not perfection, is what creates lasting change. The app gives you a clean, distraction-free space to record the habits that matter to you — nothing more.

It's designed for people who want a tool that works quietly in the background of their day. There's no streak system that punishes a missed day, no social feed, no badges or gamification mechanics designed to manufacture engagement. You open it, mark what you did, and close it. Over time, an honest picture of your patterns emerges.

Productify works well for anyone building or maintaining a personal routine — morning rituals, exercise, reading, hydration, meditation, creative practice. If you've been burned by habit apps that felt like another thing demanding your attention, Productify was built for you.
