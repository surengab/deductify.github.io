01 Capabilities

What we build

We take the software side of connected products end to end, or slot into the part of it your team doesn't want to own.

01

Connected device apps

Native iOS companion apps for Bluetooth LE, Matter, and Thread hardware. Pairing and reconnection, live control, state synchronisation, and the failure paths most apps skip — device out of range, firmware mismatch, half-completed setup.

02

Firmware & app integration

Protocol and payload design across the hardware boundary, characteristic and service layout, versioning strategy, and OTA update delivery that tolerates a user walking out of range mid-transfer.

03

Apple ecosystem depth

HomeKit and Matter accessory integration, Apple Watch companions, home and lock screen widgets, Live Activities, and background execution designed around what iOS actually permits rather than what a spec assumed.

04

Offline-first architecture

Local-first data models, command queues, and explicit conflict resolution. Connectivity is treated as intermittent by default, so behaviour in a basement or a warehouse is designed rather than discovered.

05

Telemetry & dashboards

Device data ingestion, time-series storage, alerting, and operator interfaces built around the questions support and field teams ask — which units are failing, since when, and what changed.

06

Provisioning & security

Secure onboarding, credential and key handling on-device, certificate and identity flows, and setup journeys measured by completion rate rather than feature count.

02 Process

How we work

Four phases. You can stop after any one of them, and everything produced along the way is yours.

Scope

A paid discovery week. We read the firmware, handle the hardware, and map constraints and unknowns into a written technical plan — useful even if you take it to someone else.

Prototype

The riskiest component first, almost always the connection layer. Feasibility gets settled early, on real hardware, while changing course is still cheap.

Build

Two-week cycles, each ending in a build you can install on a device. Direct access to the engineers throughout — no account manager in the middle.

Ship & hand over

App Store submission, technical documentation, and a codebase your team can take over. Ongoing support is available and optional.

03 Engagements

Ways to work together

Discovery week

Fixed scope, fixed price. A technical plan, architecture recommendation, and honest risk assessment for a connected product you're considering or already struggling with.

Project build

Defined deliverable, milestone-based. Best when the hardware exists and the software scope can be written down — companion app, dashboard, or integration layer.

Embedded engineering

We join your team for a set number of days per month, in your tooling and your standups, for roadmaps that keep moving after launch.

Technical due diligence

An independent read on someone else's connected-product codebase before you acquire it, invest in it, or take it over.

Working languages

Swift and SwiftUI on iOS, with embedded work in C and C++ and backend services in Python, Node, or Go depending on what your stack already runs. We fit the existing environment rather than arguing for a rewrite.
